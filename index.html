About Our Centre

Welcome to Perch Vocational and Technical Centre. We help students learn useful, everyday job skills. Our short courses give you the training you need to find a good job quickly and support your family.


At our school, you will spend your time practicing with real tools, learning by doing, and getting ready for real work in your community.

Student Agreement Pledge

All enrolling students pledge to honor school standard values, fulfill financial obligations, respect training regulations, and maintain accountability within the school premises.

Knowledge and Skills

Computing & Networking

Gain hands-on experience in computer hardware, operating system installation, network configurations, basic troubleshooting, and IT support essentials.

Tailoring & Design

Master pattern drafting, garment construction, sewing machine operation, textile selection, and creative fashion design techniques.

Agriculture

Learn sustainable crop management, soil health cultivation, livestock husbandry basic skills, and local agribusiness principles for sustainable farming.
