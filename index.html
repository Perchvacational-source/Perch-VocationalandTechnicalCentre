About Our Centre

Welcome to Perch Vocational and Technical Centre. We help students learn useful, everyday job skills. Our short courses give you the training you need to find a good job quickly and support your family.


At our school, you will spend your time practicing with real tools, learning by doing, and getting ready for real work in your community.
