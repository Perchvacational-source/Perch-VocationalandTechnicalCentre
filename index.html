About Our Centre

The Perch Vocational and Technical Centre is dedicated to delivering high-quality, practical training programs. We bridge the skills gap by offering accredited technical courses tailored to build immediate employment opportunities.


Our focus relies on hands-on practical environments, modern industrial knowledge, and professional values that empower community development.

Carpentry & Joinery
